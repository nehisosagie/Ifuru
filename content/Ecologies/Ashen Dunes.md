The Ashen Dunes (Volcanic Desert & Glass Fields)
A vast desert of black sand and cooling lava flows, punctuated by shards of razor-sharp obsidian. Occasionally, embers from beneath the earth ignite sandstorms, turning them into blazing infernos. Underground rivers of molten rock carry glowing organisms that resemble fireflies.

Unique Features:

Scorched husks of petrified trees that whisper when touched.

Creatures with fire-resistant fur/scales that use volcanic vents to "swim" through lava.

Glass mirages—hazy, shimmering illusions created by reflected heat.
