The Everfrost Wilds (Frozen Jungle & Ice-Choked Rivers)
A land where tropical and arctic ecosystems merge—great frost-covered trees stretch toward a perpetual aurora. Rivers flash-freeze overnight and melt by midday, causing flooding and sudden ice formations. Fire here burns blue, fueled by strange minerals in the soil.

Unique Features:

Animals with ice-coated fur/scales that use temperature shifts to camouflage.

Fire that behaves like water, able to be poured and shaped.

Glacial labyrinths that shift and change with each thaw.
