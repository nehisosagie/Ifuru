Brackish Dune Forests
A biome where coastal dunes and mangrove swamps overlap with sparse, salt-tolerant forest growth.

Core Dynamics: Wind-blown sand constantly reshapes terrain, while brackish water creates unstable root systems.

Lifeforms: Trees with dual root systems—one for salt expulsion, one for sand anchoring. Crabs and mammals dig mutual burrows.

Seasons: During high tides, the forest floor floods. In dry seasons, sand exposes fossilised roots and buried organisms.
