High-Altitude Wet Deserts
A desert located on a massive plateau with regular fog or monsoon-like mist—arid in terms of rainfall but lush in microhydration.

Water Source: Fog condensation and dew-catching vegetation (like mossy cacti, air plants).

Unique Lifeforms: Plants that drink fog and release aromatic compounds; nocturnal mammals that climb to foggy ridges to rehydrate.

Environmental Challenge: Erosion is slow but constant, caused not by rain, but wind and moisture salt creep.
