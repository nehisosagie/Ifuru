The Bone Orchard (Forest of Fossils & Undead Flora)
A strange woodland where trees grow from the remains of ancient creatures. Their bark is made of calcified bone, and their leaves are thin, membranous fossils. When the wind blows, the entire forest creaks like an old skeleton.

Unique Features:

The gravetree

Fruits that absorb memories, letting those who eat them experience the past.

Undead animals that roam between the trees, bound to the spirits of the land.

A central "heart-tree" rumored to be the skeleton of a long-forgotten god.
