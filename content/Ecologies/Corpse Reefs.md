The Corpse Reef (Bone Coral & Sunken Necropolis)
An underwater ecosystem grown from the skeletons of ancient sea creatures—and even long-dead titans. The coral here feeds on residual life essence, glowing softly. Certain areas pulse with an eerie bioluminescence, and ghostly figures swim among the ruins.

Unique Features:

Bone-grown coral that hardens like armour when disturbed.
