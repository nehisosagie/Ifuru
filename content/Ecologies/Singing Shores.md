The Singing Shores (Harmonic Tides & Coral Castles)
A coastal biome where the waves produce haunting melodies when they crash against the shore. The cliffs are covered in resonant coral, amplifying the sounds into an endless, shifting song. Locals say the sea sings warnings before storms or disasters.

Unique Features:

Crystal reefs that change color based on the emotion of the music.

Fish that "hum" in harmony, using vibrations to communicate.
