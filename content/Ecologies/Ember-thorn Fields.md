The Emberthorn Fields (Flammable Grasslands & Living Wildfire)
A sprawling savanna of red-and-gold grasses that ignite at the slightest touch. However, the flames here don’t consume—instead, they behave like living things, slithering across the fields like animals. Some even coalesce into fiery spirits.

Unique Features:

Fire-beasts made of embers and wind, hunting across the plains.

Stone trees that slowly absorb fire, preventing massive wildfires.
