>"Glory is only to be won in epic battles."

A group of mainly paladins that have taken the oath of war. Elite fighters who live for nothing but all our warfare. They revere pure combat above all else and don’t accept trickery or interference. The Oath of War always honour a request for combat. Especially one on one combat. All who take the Oath of War do so to [[Kogoun]].

Note to never get into a fight with one.
