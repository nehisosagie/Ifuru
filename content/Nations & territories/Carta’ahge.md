Started as a city state which has expanded significantly in territory. It's army mostly consists of people from occupied territory and hired soldiers. The city state which is now the capital capital sits at the ocean and has a double wall structure. The state is able to produce the best ships at a rapid pace. And the state also has a number of other successful merchant enterprises.  The state is run by the wealthy in a republic structure. Meaning it’s not about your bloodline per se, more so your wealth at the time. Of course some family lines are able to persist over generations.
