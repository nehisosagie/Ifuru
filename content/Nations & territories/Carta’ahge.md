A city state where the army is mostly consistent of people from occupied territory and hired soldiers. The city sits at the ocean and has a double wall structure. Hiding their naval fleet from the ocean front. Meaning that in order to see their might one really needs to infiltrate the city. The state is able to produce ships with rapid pace, due to a sort of assembly method. Where different parts of ships are produced ar different sites, before being combined in a finale location. The city is run by the wealthy in a sort of republic. Meaning it’s not about your bloodline per se, more so your wealth at the time. Of course some family lines are able to persist over generations.
