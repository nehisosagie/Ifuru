Nagarea has three brother kings and three capitals, all belonging to house Okuta. Each king had their own order of stone-faced guard. With a unique colour and pattern mask. 

The eldest, [[King Jabari]] has the Jade guard. He oversees the administration, making of laws, taxes and commerce. All from the city of Jiwe. 

The middle, [[King Themba]] has the Cinnabar guard. He is in charge of the judicial part, he executes and makes sure everyone keept to the laws. He also controls the police/military force. Form the city of Haki

The youngest, [[King Ayinde]] has the Lazuli guard. He takes care of diplomacy, culture and people representation. From the city of Kintana.

The kings normally rotate where the internal meetings are being held, so as to not make any one city be more dominant. Nagarea mines different special rocks and stones. Some concentrated with flow essence in them such as opal looking like rocks. But also simpler utilities from the flow essence and rocks. Rocks that allow symbols to be carved to guide the flow.  

In times of war, all the soldiers will all wear one type of stone mask. A more shining opal like mask.

Nagarea has a big religious group centred around [[The Twin hammers of Creation]]. As well as a Nyame of justice, [[Hala]].

Other cities in the nation: Mawe (stone), Kilima (hill) Daraja,

Nagarea has the The Bleeding Mountains, and a High-Altitude Wet Desert.
