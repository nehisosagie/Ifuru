Paigan, sometimes known as Paijan, is A fairly wealthy, scholarly nation. Currently ruled by the house Baraka. This house has education as a spear pillar and made their fame by making the best arcane papyrus. There are two large education centres in Paigan. Both of which attract scholars and students from different nations. Next to education, Paigan is a nation whose main export is food. There are multiple highly fertile fields stretching out across almost the whole nation. Paigan lies to the east of most of the world and has made this feature part of their success. There is good border patrol that monitors who goes in and out of the nation. Taking particular note on those who try to steal the countries farming secrets.
