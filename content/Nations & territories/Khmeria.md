The Khmer are great builders, filling the landscape with monumental temples, huge reservoirs, canals, and laying an extensive road network with all sorts of bridges. Most of the commerce is being lead by women. The empire has a a sophisticated and extensive administration going down even to the village level. Some of the most stunning temples in the world are built by the Khmer. The Khmer are festive people, with many celebrations all year round. Races, fights, fireworks, music and dancing are an integral part of their culture.
