Qorum is ruled by house Celestara. For many, the nation is quite a mysterious one as not a great deal is known about it. Qorum has tall mountain peaks and less sun hours than most other places. What is known is that the canopy house and it's branch houses have a lot of wealth. As the nation does export a variety of highly sought after magical items. Among which is the coveted Celestara blossom. Qorum also boasts an impressive number of astronomers.
