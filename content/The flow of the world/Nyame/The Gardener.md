The gardener can be worshipped in many ways. Tending to the garden of/that is life. It is believed there is a magical place called the Garden of Eternity where the gardener resides.  
Some see it as a man, others a women and some never ascribe a gender. The gardener is seen as a bringer of life, a caretaker, a shepherd of the dead and the cycle of all flows (material and energy). It is dependant on where and who worships either one aspect or all of them. Some plants seeds or see starting social enterprises as a form of worship. While more harsh followers believe there is a beauty in their killing and ensuring that there is no waste, that the materials and energy keeps flowing and being reused in the garden.
