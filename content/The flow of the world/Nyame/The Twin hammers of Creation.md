A brother and sister Nyame. Jua and Chuma are mainly seen and worshipped in [[Nagarea]]

Jua: female Nyame of mining, Figure of living stone and ore, veins of metal running through body, eyes like glowing coals

Chuma: male Nyame of carving and metalwork, Sculptor's hands holding twin tools, body adorned with every metal known, face serene and focused

Symbol: Double-headed pickaxe crossed with a sculptor's chisel
