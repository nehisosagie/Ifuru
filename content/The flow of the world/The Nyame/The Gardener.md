The gardener can be worshipped in many ways. Tending to the garden that is life. It is believed there is a magical place called the Garden of Eternity where the gardener resides.  
The gardener is seen as a bringer of life, a caretaker, a shepherd of the dead and the cycle of all flows (material and energy). 

Worshipping either one aspect or all of them varies between cultures. Some plants seeds or see starting social enterprises as a form of worship. While more harsh followers believe there is a beauty in their killing and ensuring that there is no waste, that the materials and energy keep flowing and being reused in the garden.
