The Dealer is a Nyame of exchange, chance, and binding agreements. Not good nor bad, she is more like a force of nature that happens to wear a perfectly fitted suit. She finds mortals amusing and enjoys games of chance the most. Especially if the mortal intrigues with their stakes. She always keeps her word and expects all her players to do the same. Or she and her left and right hand will ensure that they do. Remember, the house always wins.
