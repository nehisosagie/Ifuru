A brother and sister Nyame. Jua and Chuma are mainly seen and worshipped in [[Nagarea]]

Jua: female Nyame of mining, a figure of living stone and ore. Veins of metal run through her body, with eyes like glowing coals. 

Chuma: male Nyame of carving and metalwork, despite his frame he has delicate sculptor's hands. His body is adorned with every metal known and his face always serene and focused. 

Symbol: Double-headed pickaxe crossed with a sculptor's chisel
