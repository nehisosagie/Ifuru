It is believed there are 13 different paths one can follow in life, these also affect how you interact with the Flow and when walked/followed correctly can strengthen your flowing. However, straying from your path can have negative effects. 

Your path is not only about your ethics, it is also your philosophy, conviction and actions. In most cultures you get a path near adolescence through a ceremony. One can always walk a different path, however, depending on which different path you want to take it can be very difficult or easier if they lie closer together. 

Every culture has their own ceremonies, views and philosophies tied to the paths. In most places people with similar paths help each other. 

Each path has five levels of mastery:

**Novice** - Walking the path, learning basics (most people here) 
**Adept** - Understanding deeply, embodying principles 
**Master** - Can teach others, path shapes their being 
**Exemplar** - Living legend of the path, known widely 
**Embodied** - Rare as Nyame, path made flesh, almost divine. When one is reached the embodiment of a path, flowing is second nature.
